Neural Cubes

3D cellular automata in Rust + WebAssembly. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.

Rule notation

S/B/C/N: a live cell survives with a neighbor count in S, an empty cell is born with a count in B, there are C states (2 is plain alive/dead; more adds decaying states), and the neighborhood is M (26 surrounding cells) or N (6 face neighbors). Counts are lists like 5-7,12.
